Place the following font files in this directory (res://ui/fonts/), then open
the Godot editor to trigger import. DarkSagaTheme.tres references them by path.

Required files:
  Cinzel-Regular.ttf
  CinzelDecorative-Regular.ttf

Download from Google Fonts:
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel+Decorative

CrimsonText is already imported at res://assets/fonts/Crimson_Text/ — no action needed.
